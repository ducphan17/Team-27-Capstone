# Sample import texts

These UTF-8 `.txt` files are manual import corpora, outside Maven resources.
The retained literary texts were published before 1931 and are in the US public
domain as of 2026. This follows the [US Copyright Office duration guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf).
Australian availability alone is not evidence of US public-domain status.

The two files formerly labeled “Chapter 1” contain full books. `Sweeney Todd.txt`
was formerly `Untitled.txt`; other filenames have been shortened to the work's title.
Project Gutenberg Australia headers, ebook license boilerplate, and branding
footers were removed so imports learn from the literary text. Titles, author
credits, chapter headings, and the literary text are retained. These are modified
text extracts, not official PGA editions; see the [PGA modification terms](https://gutenberg.net.au/licence.html).

Source transcription credits: Mother Mason — Don Lainson and Cecilia Garcia;
The Monster Mine and The Grindwell Governing Machine — Richard Scott and Colin
Choat; Sweeney Todd and Through the Ivory Gate — Richard Scott. The supplied
Little Men and Windy McPherson's Son files did not include transcriber credits.

The three modern academic papers were removed to keep this folder limited to
public-domain texts, rather than managing separate Creative Commons conditions.
Death in the Woods and Other Stories (1933), Spring Came on Forever (1935), and
Since Yesterday (1939) were removed because US redistribution rights were not
established. No inference is made that every post-1930 work is copyrighted.
